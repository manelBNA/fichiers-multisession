Sustainability, digitalization and intelligent textile high-performance structures. The future development of textile high-performance materials is decisively taken into account at the same time. In this context, the European Commission has set up a network for the exchange of information and information on the implementation of the European Union's external policies. While in the past the maximization of mechanical parameters was the main focus, today the entire life cycle of a product, its repairability, reusability as well as the material and chemical recycling capability are increasingly being put into the focus of research. Digital methods are therefore already being used during the development process to analyse and optimize material flows, CO2 footprint, energy consumption and resource use along the entire textile value chain Modern simulation tools enable realistic predictions of the material behaviour of filaments, yarns, textiles, semi-finished products and fibre composite components even before experimental production. Digital zwillene accompany all process steps from fiber production to spinning, weaving, lichening, work and letterwarming to consolidation, final processing and quality testing.
