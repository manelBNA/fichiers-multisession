Sustainability, digitalization and intelligent textile high-performance structures. The future development of textile high-performance materials is significantly influenced by the simultaneous consideration of sustainability, digitalization and functional integration. While in the past the maximization of mechanical parameters was the main focus, today the entire life cycle of a product, its repairability, reusability as well as the material and chemical recyclability are becoming the focus of research. Digital methods are therefore already used during product development to analyse and optimize material flows, CO2 footprint, energy consumption and resource use along the entire textile value chain. Modern simulation tools allow the material behavior of filaments, yarns, textile semi-finished products and fibre composite components to be predicted realistically even before experimental production. Digital twins accompany all process steps from fiber production to spinning, weaving, lichening, working and preforming to consolidation, final processing and quality testing. The continuous recording of process data through optical sensors, force sensors, temperature measurement systems and camera-based image processing forms the basis for data-driven optimization processes. Artificial intelligence enables process parameters to be automatically adapted, quality deviations to be detected early and maintenance measures to be planned in advance. This can significantly reduce both scrap and energy and material losses, while ensuring reproducible product quality. Another research focus is the development of multifunctional textiles, combining mechanical load capacity with additional properties such as electrical conductivity, sensor integration, actuators, energy transmission or structural state monitoring. Conductive yarns, optical fibers, piezoelectric components and flexible electronics can be integrated directly into textile structures without significantly affecting their mechanical performance. This creates intelligent materials for applications in lightweight construction, mobility, medical technology, protective and functional textiles. In the long term, the combination of sustainable materials, automated manufacturing technologies and AI-based process management will form the basis for a resource-efficient, circular and highly individualized production of high-performance textile products.
