Sustainability, digitalization and intelligent textile high-performance structures. The future development of textile high-performance materials is decisive by the simultaneous consideration of the integration In the past, we have focused on maximizing mechanical characteristics, Today, research is increasingly focusing on the entire life cycle of a product, its repair capability, reusability, and its material and chemical recyclability. Digital methods are already being used during product development to analyse and optimize material flows, carbon footprint, energy consumption and resource use along the entire textile value chain. Modern simulation tools allow the material behavior of filaments, yarns, textiles, semi-finished products and fibre composite components to be predicted realistically even before experimental production. Digital twins accompany all process steps from fiber production to spinning, weaving, surfaces, work and preforming to consolidation, final processing and quality testing. The continuous recording of process data by optical sensors, force sensors, temperature measurement systems.
